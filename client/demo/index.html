How to Test Multiplayer

Experience true real-time synchronization

  1. Open this URL in two or three side-by-side browser tabs:
    http://localhost:3000/demo
  2. In each tab, keep the room name room-1 and click Join Room.
  3. Move your cursor in one tab to watch the colored live pointer glide across the other tabs.
  4. Click + Increment or type in the shared note to see instantaneous state sync.
  5. Click 🔌 Disconnect Tab in Tab 2, increment in Tab 1, then reconnect Tab 2 to verify automatic state recovery!
